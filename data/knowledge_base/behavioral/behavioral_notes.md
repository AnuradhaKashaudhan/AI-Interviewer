# Behavioral Interviewing Knowledge Base

## Topic: STAR Method Framework
- **Domain**: behavioral
- **Topic**: star_framework
- **Difficulty**: easy

### Structure
- **Situation**: Set the context by briefly describing the challenge or scenario faced.
- **Task**: Explain your specific responsibility or role in addressing the situation.
- **Action**: Outline the concrete technical and interpersonal steps you executed.
- **Result**: Quantify outcomes, impact, metrics achieved, and key lessons learned.
